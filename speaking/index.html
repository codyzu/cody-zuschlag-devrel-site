Long bio

Cody Zuschlag is Community Manager for the Xen Project, an international speaker, and a university instructor based in Annecy, France. His experience spans software engineering, developer relations, advocacy, and teaching, with open source connecting each part of his work.

His talks and workshops explore subjects across Node.js, web development, virtualization, embedded Linux, and software-defined vehicles. He enjoys turning technical ideas into approachable explanations and practical learning experiences.

At Xen, Cody brings developers, maintainers, and industry partners together, organizes events such as Xen Summit, and supports collaboration around open-source virtualization. Whether on stage, in a classroom, or working with contributors, he aims to help people understand technology and participate in the communities behind it.
